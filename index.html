How To Play

01

Place Your Orb

Take turns tapping the grid to place an orb. You can only place orbs in empty cells or cells you already own.

02

Reach Critical Mass

Every cell has a limit. Corners can hold 1 orb, edges hold 2, and the center holds 3. Adding one more pushes it over the edge!

03

Trigger The Explosion

When a cell overloads, it explodes outward! Your orbs fly into the neighboring cells, capturing them and turning enemy orbs into your own.

04

Eliminate The Competition

If another player loses all their orbs on the board, they are eliminated. The last player standing wins.

Master The Grid

Winning requires more than just tapping. Control the highly defensible corner cells early, build up a perimeter on the edges, and set up a massive, interconnected trap before your opponents realize what hit them.
